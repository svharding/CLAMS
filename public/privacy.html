CLAMS BOOKING

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

CLAMS Booking supports equipment reservations for the Nutrition & Lifestyle Lab at Memorial University. For questions about this service or your information, contact clams.booking.mun@gmail.com.

Information collected and why

We collect account names and email addresses; PI names and affiliations; equipment users’ names, email addresses and training details; primary contact information; booking dates; FOAPAL billing information; project titles, funding details, outcomes of interest, species and booking notes. These records support scheduling, training checks, administrator approval, user-fee administration and spreadsheet reporting. The service also records booking activity, email-delivery status and sign-in security information.

Public calendar and notifications

Confirmed booking dates, PI names and primary contact email addresses appear on the public calendar. Please ensure the people you name know how their details will be used. FOAPAL information, student training records and research project details are not displayed on the public calendar.

Booking emails go to the requester, primary contact, listed equipment users and the administrator as appropriate. Confirmed bookings, rescheduling and cancellations also generate notices to active registered users, containing dates, PI name, primary contact name and email, and booking status. Contact the administrator to turn off user-group notices. Sign-in and messages about your own bookings are separate from these notices.

Google account and Gmail access

Researchers sign in using an email link and do not need a Google account. Administrator Google sign-in uses the administrator’s verified email address and name. A separate authorization connects the dedicated clams.booking.mun@gmail.com mailbox with Gmail send permission, used only to send sign-in links and booking-related messages. The application does not request access to read or delete inbox messages, contacts or Google Drive files.

The Gmail authorization token is stored encrypted on the server to enable automated sending. The mailbox owner can revoke access through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings; doing so stops automated email and email-link sign-in until the mailbox is reconnected. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. CLAMS Booking’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, access and service providers

Netlify hosts the application, server functions and database service. Google processes outgoing email, and recipients’ email providers process delivered messages. Hosting and email providers may process technical logs and data outside Canada. Booking details are available in the requester’s authenticated dashboard and to authorized administrators; administrators can export records to spreadsheets. Emails and exported copies may remain with their recipients.

Cookies and retention

The application uses essential cookies for sign-in sessions, email-link browser matching and Google authorization security. It does not include advertising or analytics tracking scripts. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and sessions after seven days. Booking, training, billing and email-delivery records are retained in the system; cancelling a booking does not delete its record.

Contact the administrator to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to discuss retention. Some records may need to be retained for equipment administration, billing or applicable institutional requirements. Do not enter passwords, participant-level research data or unnecessary sensitive information in booking fields.

Changes

Updates to these practices will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
