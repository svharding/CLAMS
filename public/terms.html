CLAMS BOOKING

Terms of use

Last updated: November 7, 2026

These terms describe use of the CLAMS Booking service for the Nutrition & Lifestyle Lab at Memorial University. Questions should be directed to clams.booking.mun@gmail.com.

Reservations and charges

Reservations cover the entire CLAMS system. The minimum reservation is one week, with bookings in whole-week blocks at CAD $300 per week. The dashboard shows the calculated user fee. A submitted request is not a confirmed reservation: administrator approval is required. Dates use Newfoundland time; the displayed end date is the release date.

Provide a valid FOAPAL and ensure you are authorized to request charges against it. Billing is administered separately from this website. Discuss changes, cancellation and any billing adjustments with the administrator; cancelling in the website does not itself determine a refund or final charge.

Training and equipment care

Provide accurate training information for every equipment user. Use of the system requires administrator verification of training or an explicitly approved supervised arrangement. Students are responsible for cleaning all items that cannot be processed through the cage-washing facilities. Follow facility procedures and report equipment problems promptly.

Information and communication

Keep PI, student, contact, project and billing details accurate, and ensure you have permission to provide other people’s information. Confirmed booking dates, PI name and primary contact email are public. Booking notices are sent as described in the privacy policy. Do not submit participant-level research data or unnecessary sensitive information.

Account access

Use an email account you control. Sign-in links are personal, expire after 15 minutes and must be opened in the browser that requested them. Do not share links or attempt to access another user’s private records. Administrator functions are limited to authorized administrators.

Changes and service availability

Reservations may need adjustment for maintenance, equipment availability or facility requirements. Review the latest booking status and contact the administrator if a message is missing or a reservation appears incorrect. For urgent scheduling issues or website outages, email the booking administrator directly.
