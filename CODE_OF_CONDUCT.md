# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CLAMS Booking welcomes respectful participation from researchers, students, educators, developers and other contributors. We aim to provide a welcoming environment regardless of experience, educational background, identity, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion or personal circumstances.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and support people who are learning.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes and respect differing perspectives.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual property.
- Use fictional or anonymized examples in issues, discussions and contributions. Do not publish booking records, FOAPAL codes, personal dietary records, credentials or other private information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, personal insults, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information without permission, and retaliation against anyone raising a concern are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is explicitly representing the project in public.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to Scott Harding, project maintainer, at **sharding@mun.ca**. Include relevant links, dates and a description where possible. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Reports will be handled discreetly and shared only as needed to assess and respond to the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

If the concern involves the maintainer, or you prefer an independent route, use an appropriate reporting or support channel at your institution. This project process does not replace institutional procedures or emergency services.

## Response and enforcement

The maintainer will review reports fairly, consider the available information and respond proportionately. Responses may include a request to change behaviour, removal of inappropriate content, a warning, temporary participation restrictions or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions.

People affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the reporting address and provide additional relevant information. Retaliation for good-faith reporting is prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to follow this code, explain moderation decisions where appropriate without exposing private information, and apply it consistently. Updates will be recorded in this repository.
